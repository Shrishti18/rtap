All angles and distances **computed from the CIF** (symmetry-expanded asymmetric
unit, periodic neighbour search). Dimensionality is the rank of the lattice
translations spanned by one connected M–X component, not a visual judgement.
Angles are the six X–M–X angles inside one tetrahedron, grouped by multiplicity.

Regime tags: **REGULAR** all within 3° of 109.47 · **SPLIT-A** one pair opened
118–132° with the other four 98–106° · **SPLIT-B** one pair closed 88–102° with
the other four 112–122° · **OTHER** anything else.

Sources: `ICSD nnnnnn` = local ICSD CIF cache (FIZ Karlsruhe records, held from

## Method and limits

* Angles/distances computed directly from each CIF; no literature angle values
  were copied, so every number here is reproducible from the cited id.
* Tetrahedron test: the four nearest X within 1.15·(r_M + r_X) covalent radii,
  rejected if a fifth neighbour lies within 1.18× the fourth distance, and
  rejected if any angle exceeds 150° (that catches square-planar).
* Dimensionality: rank of lattice translations spanned by one connected M–X
  component, walked over a ±2-cell translation box.
* **Coverage limit.** The ICSD cache searched here is the 8,168-entry
  flat-band-candidate subset held from earlier work, not the whole ICSD. It
  contains 594 structures with the requested M–X pairs. Compounds absent from
  that subset were fetched individually from COD; a systematic sweep of all of
  ICSD would likely add more A₂MX₂-type chain phases.
* Electronic ground states are literature recall, **not** verified this session,
  and are marked as such.
